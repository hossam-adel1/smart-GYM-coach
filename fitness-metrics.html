How It Works

Formula and assumptions used.

BMI

Weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². A screening index, not a body-fat measurement.

BMR

Mifflin–St Jeor: 10W + 6.25H − 5A + 5 (male equation), or −161 (female equation).

TDEE

BMR × activity multiplier: 1.2, 1.375, 1.55, 1.725, or 1.9.

Macros

Protein 4 kcal/g, carbs 4 kcal/g, fat 9 kcal/g. Carbs receive remaining calories.
